**Punch Up proposal**

For my portfolio punch-up, I will be designing and developing an interactive Digital Library feature within the About Me page of my portfolio. 
This feature will showcase books I have read and books I plan to read.

**How it will work or what is the flow?**

- Users scroll to the Digital Library section.
- They see a bookshelf layout containing book spines or covers.
- When a user hovers over a book, it will visually lift and tilt in 3D space, creating the illusion that it is being picked up.
- When clicked, a popup/modal appears.
- The popup will contain:
    - Book title
    - Genre
    - A short storyline/overview
    - Why I chose to read the book
    - An interactive 3D version of the book that users can rotate or move

**How it will enhance my body of work?**

- It will allow me to merge my frontend skills with a bit of my personality.
- It will help me strengthen my CSS skills and animation skills
- It will allow me to create a more memorable experience through interactions.

**Will it be a new entry, or an interesting interaction?**

- It will be an interesting interaction added to my about me page (i might consider it a feature)
